Final Reflection: Looking Forward

Who am I becoming?

Where I started

As a technology student, I spend a lot of my time thinking about systems, instructions, and how things work. But art has always been another part of me. Even before this course, making art could help me slow down and take a break from stress. I came into GEARTS curious to see how that creative side of me would fit alongside my studies in technology.

What I discovered

I found that I genuinely enjoy making the artworks and trying the different activities. They gave me a chance to use my hands and imagination in a different way from my technology work. I also started noticing how art can hold a story or an idea, not just look nice. Creating became a welcome way to express myself and relieve stress, and I appreciated having a course that let me reconnect with that part of who I am.

What was hard

One of the biggest challenges was working within the time limits. Sometimes I needed more time to think through the insights behind an activity and decide what I wanted my work to say. It could be difficult to balance that reflection with finishing the artwork on time. I learned to make the most of the time I had, trust my first ideas enough to begin, and keep developing them as I worked.

Who I am becoming

GEARTS reminded me that being a technology student and having an arts background are not separate parts of me. I can value logic and problem-solving while also making room for imagination, emotion, and visual expression. I am leaving the course with a stronger appreciation for art and for the calm and enjoyment I find in creating. I hope to keep making time for it, even when school gets busy, because it helps me recharge and feel more connected to myself.
